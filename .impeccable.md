## Design Context

### Audience
- Internal team using the app regularly for day-to-day coordination.

### Use Cases
- Start fast with minimal friction on login and signup.
- Create an account and get into the workspace quickly.
- Return to work without unnecessary distractions.

### Brand Tone
- Friendly and welcoming.
- Clear before clever.
- Professional without feeling cold.

### Constraints
- Next.js App Router application.
- Dark theme is already established across the product.
- Preserve accessibility and readable form states.

### Visual Direction
- Warm, approachable auth experience that still feels aligned with the darker product UI.
- Strong hierarchy, concise copy, and obvious next actions.
- Distinctive enough to feel intentional, but optimized for speed and clarity rather than marketing theatrics.
