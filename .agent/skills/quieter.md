Simplify a noisy interface without making it bland.

## Focus Areas

- Remove redundant labels, helper text, and decorative elements.
- Reduce unnecessary borders, shadows, gradients, and competing accents.
- Tone down too many primary buttons or loud surfaces.
- Improve whitespace and grouping so the eye can rest.

## Method

1. Identify what is essential to the task.
2. Remove or soften anything that does not improve comprehension.
3. Preserve hierarchy using spacing, type, and one clear accent.
4. Verify that the result still feels intentional, not empty.

## Never

- Remove important affordances.
- Flatten hierarchy so much that everything feels the same.
- Replace one kind of clutter with another.

## Success Check

- Primary action is obvious.
- Visual scanning feels easier.
- The screen has fewer competing points of attention.
