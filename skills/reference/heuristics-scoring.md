# Heuristics Scoring Reference

## Severity Scale

- P0: Blocking issue; prevents successful completion
- P1: Major issue; strong friction or serious trust/accessibility problem
- P2: Minor issue; workaround exists but quality suffers
- P3: Polish issue; low impact but worth improving

## 0-4 Heuristic Score Guide

- 0: Fails badly; frequent or severe problems
- 1: Weak; multiple obvious issues
- 2: Mixed; acceptable but inconsistent
- 3: Strong; only minor issues
- 4: Excellent; best-practice quality with little to critique

## Rating Bands

- 36-40: Excellent
- 28-35: Good
- 20-27: Acceptable
- 10-19: Poor
- 0-9: Critical
