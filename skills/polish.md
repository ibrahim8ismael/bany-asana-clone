Refine a working interface into a release-ready one.

## Checklist

- Tighten spacing and alignment
- Improve hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, and success states
- Fix rough copy, truncation, and empty states
- Remove obvious placeholder behavior
- Check mobile and keyboard usability
- Ensure visual hierarchy feels intentional

## Method

1. Review the feature end to end.
2. Note small inconsistencies that compound.
3. Fix the highest-frequency rough edges first.
4. Re-test the full flow.

## Success Check

- No obvious "almost finished" moments remain.
- States feel deliberate.
- The feature feels cohesive under normal use.
